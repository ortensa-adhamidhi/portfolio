SPD Dental — Multi-Branch Operations Dashboard

A star-schema Power BI platform built on custom Power Query (M) against the Curve Dental API, surfacing production, appointments, and unscheduled treatment value across branches. All figures shown are synthetic — no real patient, provider, or financial data.

SPD Dental's front-desk teams had no visibility into treatment plans that were proposed but never scheduled, and each branch's data lived in its own silo inside the practice management system — leaving recoverable revenue untracked across locations. I built a pipeline pulling directly from the Curve Dental API via custom Power Query (M) with OAuth2 client-credentials auth, token refresh, and paginated pulls, modeled into a star schema with fact tables for appointments, ledger entries, treatment plans, and notes joined to patient, staff, clinic, and date dimensions. On top of that sit DAX measures for production, reappointment rate, treatment-plan acceptance, and no-show tracking — plus a dedicated unscheduled-treatment tracker that gives front-desk staff a direct, actionable call list.
